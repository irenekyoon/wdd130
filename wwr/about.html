Whitewater Rafting Co

Our Vision

We believe in providing safe, top-quality experiences tailored to the needs and desires of our clients. Whether it's your first time or your fiftieth time, we aim to provide the best whitewater rafting experience you've ever had.

History

Our 65 year old company got its start back in 1961, when our grandfather charged a quarter to take people down the Coquihalla River.

We now have grown into a world-class family-owned company that provides eco-experiences for our international clients who come from all walks of life.
